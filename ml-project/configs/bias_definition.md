# Definition of gender bias used in the annotation

This is the EDOS definition of sexism (Kirk et al., 2023), the same one used by the annotators of
the dataset and given to the LLM judges (`configs/prompts.yaml`). Read it before annotating and do
not change it afterwards.

A post is sexist if it contains abuse or negative sentiment directed towards women because of their
gender, or because of their gender combined with another identity attribute (for example, Black
women, Muslim women or trans women). This includes:

1. Threats or incitement to cause harm.
2. Derogation: attacks, dehumanization or sexual objectification.
3. Animosity: gendered slurs, stereotypes, backhanded compliments.
4. Prejudiced discussions: support for the discrimination or mistreatment of women.
